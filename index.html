Frequently asked questions

Is income up to ₹12 lakh really tax-free?

Under the new regime, resident individuals with taxable income up to ₹12 lakh get a Section 87A rebate (up to ₹60,000), so tax on normal income becomes zero. With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, salaried income up to ₹12.75 lakh can be effectively tax-free. Special-rate income (e.g. some capital gains) is excluded from this rebate.

Do senior citizens get higher slabs in the new regime?

No. The new regime uses the same slabs for all ages. Higher basic exemption (₹3L / ₹5L) applies only under the old regime for resident senior and super-senior citizens.

What is Health & Education Cess?

A 4% cess is levied on income tax plus surcharge under both regimes. This calculator always includes it in the total liability.

Which deductions work in the new regime?

Most Chapter VI-A deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, etc.) are not allowed. You can still claim standard deduction (₹75,000), employer NPS under 80CCD(2), family pension deduction, and interest on let-out property.

Can I switch regimes every year?

If you have no business/profession income, you can choose each year while filing. If you have business income and opt out of the new regime, switching back is restricted (generally once).

What are the new tax regime slabs for FY 2025-26?

Up to ₹4 lakh nil; ₹4–8 lakh 5%; ₹8–12 lakh 10%; ₹12–16 lakh 15%; ₹16–20 lakh 20%; ₹20–24 lakh 25%; above ₹24 lakh 30%. This applies for AY 2026-27.
